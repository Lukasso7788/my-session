# MySession semantic keyword research — 2026-10-11

This is an intent map, **not** a volume, difficulty, position, or CPC report. It covers 75 candidate phrases (25 per seed), with one primary intent per target URL. A candidate is not a recommendation to create another page. American-English wording is used for page copy.

## Evidence and method

- **SERP** means a qualitative search-result observation on 2026-10-10/11. A search for `body doubling app` surfaced native-app results alongside web products; searches for coworking rooms surfaced live room/platform pages (e.g. [EarlyBird App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/body-doubling-earlybird/id6747004367), [BuckleTime focus rooms](https://www.buckletime.com/focus), [FocusPair](https://www.focuspair.com/), [Workfrom](https://www.workfrom.com/)). This supports *intent classification only*, not demand or achievable rank.
- **SEM** means a product-grounded semantic hypothesis derived from the seed, MySession features, existing routes, and user questions. These phrases have **not** been validated as distinct searches.
- Google Search Console was attempted through the connected GSC Wizard, but it returned `payment_required` (trial/subscription expired). No Search Console impressions, clicks, positions, country splits, or query-level volume were available. Semrush/Ahrefs/Keyword Planner were not connected for this task. No metrics are inferred from competitor rankings.
- Google’s [SEO starter guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide), [JavaScript SEO guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics) and [image SEO guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images) inform the implementation. The search engine chooses snippets and title links; there is no guaranteed character-count ranking threshold. Titles here are kept short for readability.

Legend: intent `C` commercial/product, `I` informational, `N` navigational; funnel `A` awareness, `E` evaluation, `X` action. Relevance and potential are qualitative (`H` high, `M` medium). Priority `P1` implement/measure first, `P2` support in current content, `P3` research further. Every row is either SERP-observed context or an unvalidated semantic hypothesis; neither means measured search volume.
