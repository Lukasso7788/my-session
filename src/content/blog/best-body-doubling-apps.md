## The short answer

A body doubling app puts you in a live room, or on a call, with other people who are doing their own work. The best one depends on the format that gets you started. As of October 2026, these are the main options:

- **Focusmate:** booked one-to-one sessions with a matched partner.
- **MySession:** group sessions, always-open rooms, and a 1:1 queue in one place.
- **Flow Club:** host-led groups of up to nine.
- **Caveday:** guided sessions of 30 minutes to three hours.
- **FLOWN:** facilitated sessions, community sessions, and a 24/7 Drop-In.
- **CSW Study Stream:** free drop-in rooms for students.
- **FocusPair:** a free private room to share with a friend.

Most run in a browser with nothing to install; Caveday uses Zoom. Details below were checked on each provider's official site on October 11, 2026; prices and plans change, so confirm before you pay.

## What counts as a body doubling app

The useful part of [body doubling](/guides/what-is-body-doubling) is live presence: someone else is working at the same time, and you both know it. That rules out two things that are often listed alongside these apps. A solo Pomodoro timer gives you structure but no company, and a study-with-me video gives you company that cannot notice whether you showed up.

Live body doubling apps come in three formats. Booked one-to-one sessions pair you with a single partner at a set time. Hosted groups gather a small group around a host or facilitator. Drop-in rooms stay open so you can enter whenever you are ready. Most products lean on one format, and a few combine them.

## Booked one-to-one partners

**Focusmate** is the original booked format. You choose a 25, 50, or 75-minute slot, get paired with another member, say what you will work on, and check in at the end. Cameras stay on for the whole session. It suits people who need one specific person expecting them.

**MySession's 1:1 tab** offers a matching queue for 50-minute sessions, scheduled slots, and a private invite link for a friend, with the camera required. If you are weighing these two, our [MySession vs Focusmate comparison](/focusmate-alternative) goes into detail, and our list of [Focusmate alternatives](/blog/best-focusmate-alternatives) covers other options.

## Hosted and facilitated groups

**Flow Club** runs booked sessions where a volunteer host asks everyone what they are working on, plays music during the work block, and brings the group back to share progress. Groups top out at nine people. It is the most ritual-driven option and also the most expensive.

**Caveday** runs guided Caves for 20+ hours a day. A guide leads a check-in, alternates sprints and breaks, and closes with wins. Caves close 10 minutes after they start, so it rewards arriving on time.

**FLOWN** pairs facilitated sessions of one or two hours with smaller member-run community sessions and wellbeing extras such as breathwork and planning sessions.

**MySession group sessions** are run by community hosts and include intentions, focus and break stages, and a check-in. Any Free or Pro member can host one. See how the formats compare on our [Flow Club](/flow-club-alternative), [Caveday](/caveday-alternative), and [FLOWN](/flown-alternative) pages.

## Body doubling groups online you can drop into

Drop-in rooms are the closest thing to walking into a library. **MySession's always-open rooms** repeat focus and break cycles, show each person's intention and tasks, and can be joined at any point. They are busiest from about 9 AM to midnight US Eastern time. **FLOWN's Drop-In** is a silent room where everyone sets their own goal and timer, and **Caveday's Focus Lounge** is open around the clock for members.

**CSW Study Stream** is the free option for students: 24/7 rooms with cameras on, mics off, and a synced Pomodoro timer. Community chat servers can also host study rooms, but they are general chat tools rather than focus products, so the structure depends on the server.

## Free ways to body double with a friend

**FocusPair** lets you create a free video room with a built-in Pomodoro timer and share the code with someone you know. There is no sign-up and no time limit. A regular video call with a friend works too: agree on a start time, share one goal each, mute, and check in when the timer ends.

## How to choose

Ask three questions. Do you need someone expecting you at a set time? Choose a booked format. Do you want someone to lead the session? Choose a hosted or guided group. Do you mostly need to start the moment you feel ready? Choose a drop-in room. Then check two practical details: whether the camera rule fits your space, which our guide to [camera on or off](/blog/body-doubling-camera-on-or-off) covers, and whether the free option is enough for a fair test.

If you want to try a room today, MySession's [body doubling app](/body-doubling-app) runs in your browser and the Free plan covers your first 15 sessions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best free body doubling app?

For students, CSW Study Stream is free with 24/7 rooms. For working with a friend, FocusPair is free with no sign-up. Focusmate's free plan includes three sessions a week, and MySession's Free plan covers your first 15 sessions with group rooms and 1:1 matching.

### Do body doubling apps need video?

Most use video because seeing other people work is part of the effect. Rules differ: Focusmate and CSW keep cameras on, Flow Club asks for video as much as you can, and many MySession group rooms keep the camera optional unless the host requires it.

### Is a body doubling app the same as a focus timer app?

No. A focus timer structures your time, while a body doubling app adds live people working alongside you. Many body doubling apps include a shared timer, so you get both.

### Are body doubling apps good for ADHD?

Many people with ADHD describe body doubling as helpful for starting and staying on task, and the apps above are used widely by that community. They are focus tools, not treatment, and they work for people without ADHD too.
